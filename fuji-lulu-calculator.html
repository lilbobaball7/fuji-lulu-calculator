What the research means here

  • There is no defensible single “male DSH” or “female DSH” goal weight. BCS 4–5/9 is ideal; muscle condition matters too.
  • For safe feline weight loss, AAHA uses about 0.8 × RER at ideal weight as a starting point and recommends monitoring. Cats should not lose more than about 1–2% of body weight weekly.
  • The total-water range is 44–66 mL/kg of current body weight from the Merck Veterinary Manual. The progress bar treats the lower end as the daily minimum marker. Food moisture counts toward the total.
  • Wet food and increased water may help reduce lower-urinary-tract recurrence, but FIC management is multimodal and should also address stress and environment.
Urinary obstruction is an emergency. Repeated straining with little/no urine, crying, vomiting, hiding, a painful belly, or sudden lethargy warrants immediate emergency-veterinary care—do not wait for the calculator.
